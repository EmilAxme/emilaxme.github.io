FAQ

A few things you may wonder

How do I get an exact price?

The manager provides a quote from your photos, or the technician assesses the furniture on site. You approve the price before work starts.

Can you come today?

Same-day visits depend on availability. We work 24/7. Choosing a preferred time does not automatically confirm an appointment.

What does the estimate include?

The calculator adds starting prices for selected services and applies the 900 Kč minimum order. Unpriced items are quoted separately.

Is travel within Prague extra?

No, travel within Prague is free.

How does drying work?

Furniture drying can be added for 400 Kč. The technician advises on drying time based on the fabric and conditions.
